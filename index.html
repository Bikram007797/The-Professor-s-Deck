How to Play

  1. 30 cards are shuffled; each player gets 15. You only see your top card.
  2. The active player picks one of six categories.
  3. Both top cards are revealed. The higher value wins both cards, which go to the bottom of the winner's deck.
  4. The winner picks the next category.
  5. Equal values are a tie: both cards go to the pot, the same player picks again, and the next winner takes the whole pot.
  6. Lose your last card and you're out. Own all 30 cards to win.
